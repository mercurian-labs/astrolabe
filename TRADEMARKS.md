"Marks"). The code is open, and we want people to talk about it, build on it, and fork it. The Marks
are how people know a build came from us, so they work differently: Astrolabe's [code
licenses](LICENSING.md) don't grant any rights to them. This policy says what you can do without
asking.

## Uses that need no permission

- **Saying what's true.** You can use the names to refer to Astrolabe or Mercurian accurately: "works
  with Astrolabe", "an Astrolabe plugin for X", "a tutorial on Astrolabe", "built from Astrolabe's
  source code", or a review, comparison, or news article.
- **Sharing official builds.** You can redistribute unmodified official builds, as released by
  Mercurian, under their own name and logo, as long as you don't suggest you made them.
- **Linking to us.** You can use the logo to link to astrolabe.sh or to an official download page.

When you use a Mark, don't change it, don't abbreviate it or combine it with other words or logos,
and keep your own name and branding more prominent than ours.

## Forks and modified builds

The code licenses let you fork Astrolabe and change it. They don't let you call the result
Astrolabe. If you publish a fork, or distribute a build you've changed, it must:

- use a different name and a different logo, neither confusingly similar to ours, in the app, its
  icons, its listings, its package names, and its website;
- not use the Marks as part of its own name ("Astrolabe Pro", "OpenAstrolabe", "Mercurian Code");
- be described accurately if you mention where it came from, for example "based on Astrolabe's
  source code, not affiliated with Mercurian".

Changing the names, ids, and icons in your own copy is enough; you don't need to remove every
mention of Astrolabe from code, comments, or history. A private build you change and use only
inside your own organisation doesn't need renaming.

## Uses that aren't permitted

- Using a Mark, or something confusingly similar, in a domain name, product or service name,
  company name, app store listing title, or social media account name.
- Anything that suggests Mercurian makes, endorses, sponsors, or supports your product, service,
  or fork when it doesn't.
- Selling merchandise that carries a Mark.
- Using a Mark in a way that is misleading or disparaging, or that breaks the law.

## Questions and permission

If you'd like to do something this page doesn't cover, or you aren't sure whether it's allowed,
write to [legal@mercurian.ai](mailto:legal@mercurian.ai). We'll usually say yes
to uses that are accurate and don't confuse anyone.

This policy is modelled on [Mozilla's trademark policy](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/foundation/trademarks/policy/).
We may update it; the version in this repository is the current one.
