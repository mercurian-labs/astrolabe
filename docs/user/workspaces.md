# Workspaces and teams

A workspace holds the people you work with; teams inside it hold the work you
share. Your own threads need neither: they stay on your computers until you
publish them. Workspaces need an Astrolabe account.

> **Not available yet.** Astrolabe sign-in isn't live, so current builds don't
> show Workspaces.

## Start or join one

Open **Settings → Workspaces** (on mobile, **Settings → Workspaces** under
Account). Once your account is approved off the waitlist you can start one
workspace of your own; it begins with a team of the same name, which you own.
Anyone can belong to several workspaces by invitation.

Invitations go to an email address. Sign in with that address and the
invitation appears at the top of **Settings → Workspaces**, or open the link in
the email. A workspace holds five people, its owner and four more, and a
pending invitation holds a seat until it is accepted, declined or revoked.

Each workspace has an address, `/w/` followed by its short name, that opens it
in Settings.

## Roles

- **Owner**: one per workspace. Governs everything, including deleting it.
  Ownership moves only by **Hand over**, which makes the former owner an admin.
  Someone who already owns a workspace can't take a second one.
- **Admin**: manages people, invitations, teams and the workspace's policies.
- **Member**: works in every team, joins and leaves teams on their own.

Two policies decide **who can invite people** (owner and admins by default) and
**who can create teams** (everyone by default). A member allowed to invite can
invite members, not admins.

## Teams

Everyone in a workspace sees all of its teams and can join or leave them.
Whoever creates a team owns it, and team owners can name other owners from the
team. The workspace's owner and admins own every team. Only owners rename or
delete a team.

## Leaving and deleting

Anyone but the owner can leave a workspace; the owner hands it over first.
Deleting a team or a workspace asks you to type its name, and happens at once
for everyone: there is no undo. What people published there goes with it; each
person's own threads stay on their computers.
